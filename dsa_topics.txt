3. Which topics matter most for top PBC interviews?
Not every advanced competitive programming topic has equal interview value.

Here is how I would prioritize your preparation.
Tier 1 — Interview must-master
Highest priority
Arrays, strings, hashing, two pointers, sliding window, prefix sums, binary search, sorting, recursion, backtracking, linked lists, stacks, queues, trees, BST, heaps, greedy, graphs, and fundamental DP.

Target: Solve unseen Medium problems independently and explain your approach confidently.

Tier 2 — Strong candidate
Advanced interview preparation
Monotonic stack, advanced binary search, interval problems, trie, DSU, topological sorting, shortest paths, MST, advanced DP, bit manipulation, segment trees, Fenwick trees, and KMP.

Target: Solve unfamiliar Medium and selected Hard problems.

Tier 3 — Competitive programming elite
Guardian ambition
Digit DP, bitmask DP, rerooting DP, heavy-light decomposition, centroid decomposition, advanced graph algorithms, network flow, suffix automaton, FFT, NTT, computational geometry, game theory, and advanced mathematical algorithms.

Target: Solve difficult contest problems and develop algorithmic depth beyond standard interview preparation.
