B4. Modular arithmetic and fast exponentiation
This is extremely important for competitive programming.

Concepts:
- Modulo operator.
- Modular addition.
- Modular subtraction.
- Modular multiplication.
- Modular division.
- Modular exponentiation.
- Binary exponentiation.
- Modular inverse.
- Fermat's little theorem.
- Euler's theorem.
- Extended Euclidean algorithm.
- Overflow-safe multiplication.
- Modular power with large exponents.
Understand these properties:
(a+b)\bmod m

(a\cdot b)\bmod m

a^b\bmod m
