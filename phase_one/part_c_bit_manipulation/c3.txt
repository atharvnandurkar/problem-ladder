C3. XOR properties and applications
This is one of the most important subtopics.

Understand these identities:

a\oplus a=0

a\oplus 0=a

a\oplus b=b\oplus a

(a\oplus b)\oplus c=a\oplus(b\oplus c)

Learn:
- XOR cancellation.
- Find the unique element.
- Find two unique elements.
- XOR of a range.
- Prefix XOR.
- XOR of subarrays.
- XOR partitioning.
- XOR basis (advanced).
